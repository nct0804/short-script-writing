# Lesson 6 · Cheat sheets

## Fact on one page
**Sentence 1:** the strange deed, past tense, no name, ≤ 17 words. "He [did the strange thing]."
**Shape (Fact story, 55-65 s, 200-245 words, 3.7 words/s):**
1. Hook (deed) → 2. Setup ("You see," optional; present tense) → 3. Escalation (So / But / Then, 4-8 beats, the mechanism
   planted on screen) → 4. Mystery ("But why?") → 5. Reveal ("Well," / "You see,"; one mechanism; say what's real) →
   6. Payoff (twist or visual; ends on the last word)
**Formats:** Fact story (default) · Fact event (a dated real event, all sourced) · Fact explainer ("If you …", 35-50 s)
**Language:** sentences ~14 words with a long-short rhythm · roles, not names · unattributed one-liners · never
narrate the picture · numbers, not adjectives
**Truth:** facts table, every line "fact" or "dramatized" · two good sources per mechanism
**Must-haves (7):** deed first · sourced and marked · one mechanism · strong last line · length fits 3.7 w/s · every
line showable · original
**Score:** Hook 25 · Escalation 20 · Mechanism 20 · Payoff 15 · Language 10 · Truth 10 → ship at 85
**Captions:** one bold word at a time

## Fable on one page
**Sentence 1:** the rule. "In my town, if you [everyday action], [magic result]."
**Fable story (warm, ~2:00, 450-600 words, 4.3 words/s):**
1. The rule + price + stake → 2. Proof montage (3-6 cases, names + numbers, one plant) → 3. "Me?" (the misfit) →
4. Loved one + ritual → 5. Pressure (countdown, adults fail) → 6. The turn ("That's when I understood.") →
7. Sacrifice through the rule → 8. Callback (the ritual, changed)
**Fable story (eerie):** rule → tonight it broke (by sentence 6) → world facts that raise the danger → the one who
depends on the narrator → the turn → a costly choice → a dread sting. Present tense, cinematic.
**Fable gag (36 s, 180-195 words, 5.2 words/s):** premise → three escalations → one-line dialogue flip
**Rule tests:** everyday action · visible in frame 1 · a price · a loophole
**Moral Fable:** value → the everyday action that tests it → a rule that shows its opposite · the misfit has the value
too much · the turn re-reads the rule · the sacrifice acts it out · never say the moral
**Language:** sentences ~6 words · a one-word sentence every 8-10 lines · 3+ deadpan jokes · sound words · dialogue
under 10 words · no feelings narrated before the turn
**Must-haves (8):** rule first · visible rule · price early · misfit solves it · loved one + ritual · callback ending ·
length fits the pace · original
**Score (story):** Hook 20 · Montage 15 · Heart 15 · Turn 15 · Sacrifice 10 · Ending 10 · Language 10 · Comment fuel 5
**Score (gag):** Hook 30 · Escalation 30 · Flip 30 · Language 10 → ship at 85
**Captions:** bright 2-4 lowercase words · eerie one yellow word · gag top chunks

## Both
- No intro, no "did you know", no "wait for it", no call to action, no "follow for part 2". End on the last word.
- Frame 1 = sentence 1. One shot per noun. Text props carry exposition.
- Master at about −14 LUFS; music ~10 LU under the voice; silence right before the reveal or the big gag.
- Patterns, never copies. Never name the creators you studied.
