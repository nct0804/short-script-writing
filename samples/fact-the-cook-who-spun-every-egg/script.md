# The Cook Who Spun Every Egg

**Type:** Fact · **Format:** Fact story · **Target length:** 1:05 · **Words:** 239 (≈3.7 words/s)
**Logline:** On his first diner shift a new cook spins every egg instead of cracking them, because a spin tells a raw
egg from a boiled one without breaking it, and the last boiled egg stands up on its tip.
**Title options:** 1. The Cook Who Spun Every Egg (recommended) · 2. Why Chefs Spin Eggs · 3. He Spun Every Egg in the Diner

Hook options: 1. "He spun every egg in the diner before he cracked a single one." (recommended: the deed, picturable,

eggs, and spent his whole first shift spinning them."

## Script

### 1. Hook · 0:00–0:04 · 13 words
He spun every egg in the diner before he cracked a single one.

### 2. Setup · 0:04–0:12 · 31 words
You see, it's his first shift, and someone just dumped the boiled eggs and the raw eggs into the same crate. And the
lunch rush wants egg salad in twenty minutes.

### 3. Escalation · 0:12–0:38 · 98 words
So he sets an egg on the counter and gives it a spin. The cooks stop flipping pancakes to stare. Is he hypnotizing the
breakfast? But he doesn't look up, he just spins, watches, and drops each egg into one of two bowls, faster and faster,
like a card dealer. Then the boss storms in, because table nine has waited so long, they've started reading the menu
out loud to each other. So he grabs one more egg, spins it, stops it dead with one finger, lets go, and the egg starts
turning again all by itself. Raw.

### 4. Mystery · 0:38–0:41 · 9 words
But how does a spin tell you what's inside?

### 5. Reveal · 0:41–0:57 · 61 words
Well, a boiled egg is solid, so the shell and the inside turn together, smooth as a top. A raw egg is liquid inside,
so it sloshes and drags and the egg wobbles. Stop the shell, and the liquid keeps swirling, so it pushes the shell
right back into a spin. And in real life, chefs really check eggs this way.

### 6. Payoff · 0:57–1:05 · 27 words
And for the last boiled egg, he spins it as hard as he can, until it slowly rises onto its tip and keeps spinning,
standing straight up.
