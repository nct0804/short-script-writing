# Sample · Fact story: The Cook Who Spun Every Egg

`final.mp4` (66 s, 1080x1920) · `script.md` (the full script with sources, shot list, captions, score)

## How it was made
1. **Script** with `fact-script`. Prompt: *"Fact script: a new diner cook spins every egg before cracking it."*
   - The skill picked **Fact story** mode: an unnamed cook acts out a real kitchen test.
   - It offered three deed hooks and recommended *"He spun every egg in the diner before he cracked a single one."*
   - It sourced every claim (Scientific American, Science Buddies, a chef's demonstration, *Nature* 2002) and marked the
     diner story **dramatized**.
   - Score: 92/100.
2. **Voice:** an adult storyteller voice (ElevenLabs), lightly sped up and with held pauses trimmed: 65.8 s at 3.64
   words/s (target 3.7).
3. **Picture:** a cartoon kitchen, dining room and chalkboard; 14 shots, about 40 camera set-ups, glides and
   crossfades. Text props carry the setup: the crate stencil "BOILED + RAW" with an "OOPS" note, the "EGG SALAD x40 ·
   12:00" ticket, the clock at 11:40. The reveal is a chalkboard diagram; the payoff, the egg rising onto its tip.
4. **Captions:** one bold word at a time; RAW, BOILED, SOLID, LIQUID, SPIN in colour.
5. **Sound:** a whirr on every spin, a tap on every stop, a door bang for the boss, a diner murmur. Bouncy music that
   drops to silence before "Raw.", and a pizzicato button. Mastered at −14 LUFS.

## What to learn from it
- **The deed hook works without a stake:** "He spun every egg …" is strange enough. The stake (the lunch rush)
  arrives in sentence 3, the one point the score card marks down.
- **Show the mechanism before you explain it:** the finger stops the egg and it starts turning again, *then* the
  mystery question, *then* "Well, …". The reveal lands because the viewer has already seen it.
- **"In real life, chefs really check eggs this way."** One line tells the viewer which part is real.
- **A true payoff beats an invented one:** the rising egg is real physics, and it's the best image in the video.
