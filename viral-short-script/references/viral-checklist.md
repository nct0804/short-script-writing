# Viral checklist

Score every draft before showing it. Fix anything failing and re-score. Show the card under the script.

## Must-haves (any ✗ = rewrite that part before showing)

- [ ] First sentence states the rule or premise. No intro, question, "wait for it" or CTA.
- [ ] The rule is an everyday action with a visible consequence that can be frame 1.
- [ ] A price or catch is stated within the first 10 seconds.
- [ ] Narrator's misfit trait is planted early and is what solves the crisis.
- [ ] Loved one (sibling, grandparent, parent) with a repeated ritual, planted before 1:00.
- [ ] Last line echoes the first line or the ritual, changed. Then the script ends.
- [ ] Word count fits the pace: long 450 to 600 words (4.3 w/s); gag 180 to 195 (5.2 w/s).
- [ ] Original: no plot, rule, character or line lifted from another creator.

## Score (100 points; ship at 85+)

| Area | Points | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 20 | Rule in sentence 1, stake by sentence 3, strangest image first, under 5 s. |
| Proof montage | 15 | 3 to 6 cases, each with a name and a number, at least 2 laugh lines, one plant. |
| Heart | 15 | Loved one is small or old and specific; ritual line is short and repeatable; feelings shown, not said. |
| Turn | 15 | "That's when I understood" re-reads the rule; it's surprising but fair on a rewatch. |
| Sacrifice | 10 | Flaw used *through* the rule; a real cost; a plant pays off. |
| Ending | 10 | Callback in 1 or 2 lines; a joke inside the warmth or a calm sting. |
| Language | 10 | Sentences about 8 words, a one-word sentence every few lines, at least 3 deadpan jokes, sound words, dialogue under 10 words. |
| Comment fuel | 5 | One clear loophole or a mystery viewers can solve early. |

For gags, score Hook 30, Escalation (three beats, each bigger) 30, Flip 30, Language 10.

## Card format

```
Must-haves: 8/8 ✓
Hook 19/20 · Montage 14/15 · Heart 14/15 · Turn 13/15 · Sacrifice 9/10 · Ending 10/10 · Language 9/10 · Comment fuel 5/5
Total: 93/100
Weakest spot: [one line on what would push it higher]
```

## Common fixes

- **Hook is slow:** cut everything before the rule; move the stake into sentence 2.
- **Montage isn't funny:** add a public authority embarrassment, a family mishap, a job that adapted, and a deadpan button line.
- **Turn feels random:** plant the clue in the montage as a joke, so it reads as a callback.
- **Ending is long:** keep only the ritual line and one sting.
- **Too many words:** cut adjectives and feelings first; keep nouns, numbers and actions.
