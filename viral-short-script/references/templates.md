# Templates and output format

## Long world-rule story (about 2:00, 480 to 560 words at 4.3 words/s)

```
TITLE (caps, first person, ends in "..."): MY ______ ______...

[0:00-0:05] 1. THE RULE  (~20 words)
In this town, if you ______, ______.
You see, ______.                      (one line on how it works)
But if you ______, ______.            (the price)  /  or: "Mine ______." (narrator is the exception)
[+ personal stake: "My ______ has been ______ for ___ years."]

[0:05-0:30] 2. YOU SEE... PROOF MONTAGE  (~100 words, 3-6 one-to-two-line cases)
- [Authority figure] ______ in public. (name + number)
- [Family member] ______ at home.
- [Ordinary job] has adapted in a silly way: ______.
- [Cold fact, PLANT]: "______ tried in [year]. ______ still ______."
- [Deadpan button]: "Nobody's in a hurry to ______."

[0:30-0:40] 3. ME?  (~40 words)
Me? I'm the ______-est kid in town. The kids call me "______".
[Show the weakness as a joke, with one example.]

[0:40-1:00] 4. LOVED ONE + RITUAL  (~90 words)
My ______ (sister / grandpa / Nana), ___ years old. Every ______, we ______.
[The ritual line or gesture: "______"]   <- returns in beat 8
[The secret: an old loss tied to the rule, with a name and a year.]

[1:00-1:30] 5. PRESSURE  (~110 words)
Then ______ (selfish authority / disaster).
[Countdown: "On Monday ______. On Tuesday ______." or "6:41 ... 6:55 ... 7:09"]
[An adult tries and fails: too short, too late, too scared.]
[One deadpan joke inside the danger.]

[1:30-1:40] 6. THE RULE TURNS AROUND  (~40 words)
That's when I understood. The rule never meant ______. It meant ______.

[1:40-1:55] 7. SACRIFICE THROUGH THE RULE  (~70 words)
[Narrator uses the misfit trait THROUGH the rule.]
[Sensory moment inside the rule: "the ______-est ______ I've ever felt".]
[Pay off the beat-2 plant.]
It cost me ______.
[One short quoted line that heals the old wound.]

[1:55-2:05] 8. CALLBACK  (~25 words)
[Ritual or first line returns, changed.] [One short sting or joke.]
```

## 36-second gag (180 to 195 words at 5.2 words/s)

```
TITLE (caps + one emoji): ______ ______ 😳

[0:00-0:04] PREMISE (~20 words)
My crush / best friend / girlfriend's dad ______. [literal reading or secret power]

[0:04-0:30] ESCALATION x3 (~140 words)
1. [First attempt, small, funny.]
2. [Bigger, with a number.]
3. [Biggest, almost works, then goes wrong.]

[0:30-0:36] FLIP (~25 words)
[Reversal in one beat.] "[One line of dialogue, under 10 words, that turns the premise around.]"
```
