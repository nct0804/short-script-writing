# Fable idea engine

## Turning a seed into a rule

Whatever the user gives you, push it through these questions until one impossible rule falls out.

| Seed type | Ask | Example seed → rule |
|---|---|---|
| A single word ("rain") | What everyday action could cause it? What would it do that it can't? | rain → "In my town, it only rains on people who lied that day." |
| A feeling ("loneliness") | What everyday action shows it? Make that action visibly magic. | loneliness → "In this town, every day nobody says your name, you get a little more see-through." |
| An object ("keys") | What if it had a rule of its own? | keys → "Every key in my town opens one door, but nobody knows which." |
| A "what if" | Make it a law for everyone, then find who it hurts. | "what if you could pause time" → "The whole town freezes for one minute at noon, except whoever is crying." |
| A character ("a grumpy neighbour") | What rule would make them the villain, or secretly the hero? | → "Any kite that lands in Mr. Halloran's yard is his forever." |
| A plot ("kid saves grandma") | What rule is grandma trapped by? | → "In my town, if you forget someone's birthday, they forget you." |
| A joke or a romance | Probably a Fable gag: a literal reading or a secret power. | "my crush is cold" → "My crush's touch freezes things. I live in Florida." |

### The four tests (all must pass)
1. **Everyday action.** Anyone watching does it daily: talk, sleep, walk, lie, laugh, eat, wave, say goodnight, take a
   photo, kick a ball, cry, blink.
2. **Visible in frame 1.** You can picture the strangest image of the story as the first shot. If you can only
   explain it, it fails.
3. **A price.** Breaking or misusing the rule costs something big (stuck, stone, forgotten, gone, swapped).
4. **A loophole.** One question viewers will argue about in the comments ("why not just close your eyes?").

### Then find the misfit and the heart
- **Misfit trait = the rule's blind spot.** If the rule punishes looking, the hero can't look (scared). If it rewards
  loudness, the hero is quiet. If it needs speed, the hero is slow. The trait is a joke in beat 3 and the key in beat 7.
- **Loved one with a ritual:** a little sibling (4-7), a grandparent, a parent. Give them a repeated line or gesture
  ("Still here?", a whistle, a song, a hand on the window) and, for grandparents, an old loss tied to the rule.
- **The turn:** what does the rule *really* mean? Common turns: the rule protects from something worse; the rule was
  never magic, just obeyed; the "monster" is the victim; time moves differently than everyone thinks; the punishment
  can be chosen on purpose.

### Warm or eerie?
- Funny, warm or bittersweet idea → **warm Fable story** (classic 8 beats), bright look.
- Eerie or epic idea (a watcher, a deadline at sunset, something under or behind) → **eerie Fable story**
  (crisis-first), cinematic look.
- A joke or a crush → **Fable gag**.

## Lenses for pitching from nothing
Use 5 different lenses for 5 pitches so they don't feel alike.

1. **Body or sense rule:** blinking, breathing, sneezing, hunger, sleep, shadows, reflections.
2. **Words rule:** what you say becomes physical, comes back, is counted, freezes, grows.
3. **Time rule:** something happens every sunset, every 10 years, on your 10th birthday, at 3:33.
4. **Value swap:** something cheap is precious, something precious is trash.
5. **Place rule:** houses, walls, bridges, stairs, the lake, the edge of town, the school.
6. **Kindness or karma rule:** what you do to others happens to you, letting people go first, saying thank you.
7. **Family rule:** names, goodnights, photos, birthdays, hugs, chores.
8. **Eerie rule:** a watcher, a collector, a thing under / behind / inside, the last of something.
9. **Gag lens:** a crush with a power, a literal reading, a bet, a rule between best friends, a deal with a dad.

the heart.

| 2 | MY LITTLE BROTHER WON'T LET GO OF HIS BALLOON… | "In my town, when you let go of a balloon, it carries one memory away with it." | Kid who can't hold anything; little brother keeps a balloon for Mom. Turn: the sky keeps memories safe. (Written up in `examples.md`.) |

| 5 | MY GRANDPA FEEDS THE SNOW… | "In my town, every snowflake is a word somebody never said." | Kid who blurts everything; Grandpa shovels the same patch every winter. |
| 6 | PENNIES ARE WORTH MORE THAN HOUSES | "In this town, one penny buys a house, and nobody has one." | Kid who finds a jar of them in Grandpa's shed; spending one means losing what it was saved for. |
| 7 | EVERY HUG COSTS A YEAR… | "In my town, every hug you give takes a day off your life, so nobody hugs." | Clingy little sister; the oldest woman in town turns out to be the biggest hugger. |

| 12 (gag) | DAD'S ONE RULE ☝️ | "Her dad has ONE rule: never beat him at chess." | He throws three games, worse each time; flip: the dad has been throwing them too. |
| 13 (gag) | MY CRUSH'S LAUGH GROWS FLOWERS 🌷 | "Every time my crush laughs, flowers grow wherever she looks." | Thirty days of failed jokes; a disaster finally does it. (The animated sample in `samples/`.) |

… (5 pitches: at least 3 stories (one warm, one bittersweet, one eerie) and 1-2 gags)
