# Fact checklist

Score every draft before showing it. Fix anything failing and re-score. Show the card under the script.

## Must-haves (any ✗ = rewrite that part before showing)
- [ ] Sentence 1 is the deed or the paradox as plain fact. No intro, greeting, "did you know" or call to action.
- [ ] Every claim is in the facts table with a source, and every line is marked fact or dramatized. A dramatized story
      is generic (no real names, dates or places), and the reveal says what is real. Low-confidence facts are flagged or
      cut.
- [ ] One mechanism: said after a question + "Well," / "You see,", or shown so clearly by the payoff that saying it
      would be redundant. An everyday analogy is a plus, not a must.
- [ ] The last line is a twist, a visual payoff, the rule or an ironic callback. Nothing after it, and no question to
      the viewer.
- [ ] Length: Fact story 200-245 words (55-65 s); Fact event 170-250; Fact explainer 140-200; words ÷ 3.7 within the
      target seconds.
- [ ] Every sentence can be shown literally, and no line narrates what's already on screen.
- [ ] Original telling: no lines or structure-for-structure retelling from another creator's video.

## Score (100 points; ship at 85+)
| Area | Points | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 25 | The deed or paradox in ≤ 17 words, instantly picturable, makes you ask "why would he?"; sentence 2 sets the world |
| Escalation | 20 | 4-8 beats chained with So / But / Then, each bigger; reactions as unattributed one-liners; the mechanism planted visibly; at least one exaggeration joke |
| Mechanism | 20 | One idea, plain words, after a mystery question; or shown by the payoff. Extra credit for an analogy the viewer has *felt* |
| Payoff | 15 | A twist or a visual consequence, short, lands on the last word; ideally turns the hook around |
| Language | 10 | ~14-word median with a long-short rhythm, contractions, present tense for the story, roles not names, specific numbers, no adjective piles |
| Truth | 10 | All facts high confidence or clearly hedged; nothing invented for the joke |

## Card format
```
Must-haves: 7/7 ✓
Hook 22/25 · Escalation 18/20 · Mechanism 19/20 · Payoff 14/15 · Language 9/10 · Truth 10/10
Total: 92/100
Weakest spot: [one line on what would push it higher]
```

## Common fixes
- **The hook is slow:** cut everything before the deed; move the place and the stakes to sentence 2.
- **The escalation is flat:** add a reaction line, a number that grows, and one real detail that's funny on its own.
- **The explanation sprawls:** keep one mechanism; cut the second idea.
- **The payoff is soft:** make the mechanism fire on screen, or turn the consequence back on the people in the hook.
- **Too long:** cut adjectives and the second example first; keep nouns, numbers, actions.
