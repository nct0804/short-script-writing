# Fact templates and output format

Three templates: the **Fact story** (default), the **Fact event** (a documented real event) and the **Fact explainer**
(science with no person in it). Timestamps come from words ÷ 3.7.

## Fact story (55-65 s, ~200-245 words)

```
TITLE: what he did, or why it works, under 50 characters ("Why [people] [do odd thing]", "The [Person] Who [Deed]")

[0:00-0:04] 1. HOOK  (6-17 words, PAST tense)
He [did the strange / dumb / mean thing].

[0:04-0:09] 2. SETUP  (1-2 lines, switch to PRESENT tense; "You see," optional)
You see, [the situation, the stakes, the deadline].

[0:09-0:40] 3. ESCALATION  (4-8 lines chained with So / But / Then, ~110 words)
- [Someone reacts: an unattributed one-liner, < 10 words.]
- So [he does it anyway, bigger].
- But [the doubters / the rival / the boss push back]. [An exaggeration joke as a comparison.]
- Then [a sign that something is building: plant the mechanism here, visibly].
- [The biggest moment: one run-on sentence of 20+ words that speeds up.]

[0:40-0:43] 4. MYSTERY  (1 short line)
"But why?" / "Wait, what?" / "But how does [X] tell you [Y]?"

[0:43-0:55] 5. REVEAL  (1-4 lines, ~45 words)
Well, [the real mechanism in plain words]. / You see, in real life, [the real practice].
(optional) Ever [an everyday experience]? [How it maps onto the mechanism.]

[0:55-0:62] 6. PAYOFF  (1-2 lines)
[The twist lands, or the mechanism fires on screen.] (Nothing after it.)
```

Variant: skip 4-5 and let the payoff *show* the mechanism when it's visible (things expand, rise, fall, burn).

## Fact event (45-70 s, 170-250 words) — a documented real event, every detail sourced

```
TITLE: the paradox, under 60 characters ("The [Thing] That [Did the Impossible]")

[0:00-0:04] 1. HOOK  (10-16 words)
[Number] [who] [did the impossible / dumb thing] [with stakes].

[0:04-0:10] 2. SETTING  (1-2 lines, ~20 words)
It's [year], and [the place / the people] [a concrete detail + pride or stakes].

[0:10-0:30] 3. ESCALATION  (3-6 lines, ~75 words, each bigger than the last)
- [The first sign something's off.]
- [Somebody doubts or laughs: a short in-character line.]
- [An exaggeration joke that is a real fact.]
- [Bigger.] [Biggest.]
(optional) MYSTERY: "Wait, [the question the viewer now has]?"

[0:30-0:34] 4. SNAP  (1 short line)
Then [the moment it breaks or flips].

(optional) DEADPAN DOUBLE (2 lines): "Luckily, [x]. Unluckily, [x]."  (the same fact read two ways)

[0:34-0:48] 5. "YOU SEE," + ANALOGY  (~50 words)
You see, [the one real mechanism, in plain words].
Ever [an everyday experience]? [How it maps onto the mechanism.]

[0:48-0:55] 6. CONSEQUENCE  (1 line)
[What changed because of it: a rule, an invention, a law, a habit.]

[0:55-0:60] 7. PAYOFF  (1 line)
[An ironic twist or callback.] (Nothing after it.)
```

## Fact explainer (35-50 s, 140-200 words)

```
[0:00-0:04] HOOK: If you [everyday action], [surprising physical result].
[0:04-0:10] You see, [the setup] / Imagine you're [situation].
[0:10-0:35] 3-5 escalating steps in the second person, each a consequence of the last.
[0:35-0:45] The mechanism, in plain words.
[0:45-0:50] A one-line button. (No question to the viewer.)
```
