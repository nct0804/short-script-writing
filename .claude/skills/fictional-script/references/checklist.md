# Fable checklist

Score every draft before showing it. Fix anything failing and re-score. Show the card under the script.

## Must-haves (any ✗ = rewrite that part before showing)
- [ ] Sentence 1 states the rule or premise. No intro, question, "wait for it" or call to action.
- [ ] The rule is an everyday action with a visible consequence that can be frame 1.
- [ ] A price or catch is stated within the first 10 seconds (eerie: "today it broke" by sentence 6).
- [ ] The narrator's misfit trait is planted early and is what solves the crisis. *(stories)*
- [ ] A loved one (sibling, grandparent, parent) with a repeated ritual, planted before 1:00. *(stories)*
- [ ] The last line echoes the first line or the ritual, changed. Then the script ends.
- [ ] Word count fits the pace: story 450-600 words (4.3 words/s); gag 180-195 (5.2 words/s).
- [ ] Original: no plot, rule, character or line taken from another creator.
- [ ] Moral Fables: the value is shown in the sacrifice (or the flip) and felt in the callback, never said: no
      "the moral of the story", no lecture, the value's name not in the narration.

## Score: Fable story (100 points; ship at 85+)
| Area | Points | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 20 | Rule in sentence 1, stake by sentence 3, strangest image first, under 5 s |
| Proof montage (eerie: world facts) | 15 | 3-6 cases, each with a name and a number, at least 2 laugh lines (eerie: each raises the danger), one plant |
| Heart | 15 | The loved one is small or old and specific; the ritual line is short and repeatable; feelings shown, not said |
| Turn | 15 | "That's when I understood" re-reads the rule; surprising but fair on a rewatch |
| Sacrifice | 10 | The flaw is used *through* the rule; a real cost; a plant pays off |
| Ending | 10 | Callback in 1-2 lines: a joke inside the warmth, or a calm sting |
| Language | 10 | ~6-word sentences, a one-word sentence every 8-10 lines, at least 3 deadpan jokes, sound words, dialogue under 10 words |
| Comment fuel | 5 | One clear loophole, or a mystery viewers can solve early |

## Score: Fable gag (100 points; ship at 85+)
| Area | Points | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 30 | The premise in sentence 1, visible in frame 1, the stake by sentence 3 |
| Escalation | 30 | Three beats, each bigger, each with a specific number or object; at least one sound word |
| Flip | 30 | One line of dialogue under 10 words that turns the premise around; ideally echoes the first line |
| Language | 10 | ~6-word sentences, fast, no feelings narrated |

**Moral Fables** use the story score; the Turn (15) and the Sacrifice (10) carry the moral: full marks only when the
turn re-reads the rule in story words and the sacrifice acts the value out at a real cost.

## Card format
```
Must-haves: 8/8 ✓
Hook 19/20 · Montage 14/15 · Heart 14/15 · Turn 13/15 · Sacrifice 9/10 · Ending 10/10 · Language 9/10 · Comment fuel 5/5
Total: 93/100
Weakest spot: [one line on what would push it higher]
```

## Common fixes
- **The hook is slow:** cut everything before the rule; move the stake into sentence 2.
- **The montage isn't funny:** add a public authority embarrassed, a family mishap, a job that adapted, and a deadpan
  button line.
- **The turn feels random:** plant the clue in the montage as a joke, so it reads as a callback.
- **The ending is long:** keep only the ritual line and one sting.
- **Too many words:** cut adjectives and feelings first; keep nouns, numbers and actions.
- **Sentences too long to read aloud fast:** split at every "and", "but", "so"; aim for 6 words.
