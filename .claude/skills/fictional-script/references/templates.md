# Fable templates and output format

Three templates: the **warm Fable story** (classic 8 beats), the **eerie Fable story** (crisis-first) and the **Fable
gag**. Timestamps come from words ÷ pace (4.3 words/s for stories, 5.2 for gags).

## Warm Fable story (about 2:00, 480-560 words at 4.3 words/s)

```
TITLE (caps, first person, ends in "…"): MY ______ ______…

[0:00-0:05] 1. THE RULE  (~20 words)
In this town, if you ______, ______.
(optional) You see, ______.          (one line on how it works)
But if you ______, ______.           (the price)  /  or: "Mine ______." (the narrator is the exception)
[+ personal stake: "My ______ has been ______ for ___ years."]

[0:05-0:30] 2. PROOF MONTAGE  (~100 words, 3-6 one-to-two-line cases)
- [An authority figure] ______ in public. (name + number)
- [A family member] ______ at home.
- [An ordinary job] has adapted in a silly way: ______.
- [Cold fact, the PLANT]: "______ tried it in [year]. ______ still ______."
- [Deadpan button line.]

[0:30-0:40] 3. ME?  (~40 words)
Me? I'm the ______-est kid in town. The kids call me "______".
[Show the weakness as a joke, with one example.]

[0:40-1:00] 4. LOVED ONE + RITUAL  (~90 words)
My ______ (sister / grandpa / Nana), ___ years old. Every ______, we ______.
[The ritual line or gesture: "______"]   <- returns in beat 8
[The secret: an old loss tied to the rule, with a name and a year.]

[1:00-1:30] 5. PRESSURE  (~110 words)
Then ______ (a selfish authority / a disaster).
[Countdown: "Monday, ______. Tuesday, ______." or "6:41 … 6:55 … 7:09"]
[An adult tries and fails: too short, too late, too scared.]
[One deadpan joke inside the danger.]

[1:30-1:40] 6. THE TURN  (~40 words)
That's when I understood. The rule never meant ______. It meant ______.

[1:40-1:55] 7. SACRIFICE THROUGH THE RULE  (~70 words)
[The narrator uses the misfit trait THROUGH the rule.]
[A sensory moment inside the rule: "the ______-est ______ I've ever felt".]
[Pay off the beat-2 plant.]
It cost me ______.
[One short quoted line that heals the old wound.]

[1:55-2:05] 8. CALLBACK  (~25 words)
[The ritual or first line returns, changed.] [One short joke or sting.]
```

## Eerie Fable story (crisis-first, 1:45-2:30, present tense)

```
TITLE: the rule or the place, in caps: THE LIGHTHOUSE THAT COUNTS US / or a teaser ending in "…"

[0:00-0:06] 1. THE RULE  (1-2 lines, no "In this town" needed)
Every sunset, ______. / We only ______ when ______.

[0:06-0:20] 2. TODAY IT BROKE  (sentence 2-6)
Tonight, ______. / This morning, ______.   (the narrator is on the wrong side of the rule)

[0:20-0:60] 3. WORLD FACTS THAT RAISE THE DANGER  (short lines, names, numbers)
- [How the rule has worked for ___ years.]
- [Who broke it, and what happened. A number.]
- [The one thing nobody is allowed to do.]

[0:60-1:20] 4. THE ONE WHO DEPENDS ON THE NARRATOR
[A 4-year-old, a sister, a grandparent: why the narrator can't just run.]

[1:20-1:45] 5. THE TURN
[What the rule really is. "It wasn't ______. It was ______."]

[1:45-2:10] 6. A CHOICE THAT COSTS
[The narrator pays to protect them.]

[2:10-2:20] 7. DREAD STING
[The threat turns toward the narrator, in one calm line.]
```

## Fable gag (36 s, 180-195 words at 5.2 words/s)

```
TITLE (caps + one emoji): ______ ______ 😳

[0:00-0:04] PREMISE  (~20 words)
My crush / best friend / her dad ______. [a literal reading, or a secret power]
[The stake in sentence 2-3: "I've never ______."]

[0:04-0:30] ESCALATION x3  (~140 words; day cards keep it moving: "Day one … Day nine … Day thirty")
1. [First attempt: small, funny.]
2. [Bigger, with a number.]
3. [Biggest: it almost works, then goes wrong. Sound words: Splat. Pfffft.]

[0:30-0:36] FLIP  (~25 words)
[The reversal in one beat.] "[One line of dialogue, under 10 words, that turns the premise around.]"
```
